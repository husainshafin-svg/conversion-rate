Welcome to the inaugural post of Conversion Rate, a blog for deconstructing financials, go-to-market strategies, and the examples of both across all types and genres of games. In this first deep dive that this blog will cover, we will be tackling the issue of leaks considering *Dragon Ball Legends*. I was initially struggling what game or genre to cover first, but thanks to *Dragon Ball Legends* latest mishap, I was able to determine what my first blog post should center on. All thanks to YouTube prominently displaying...     

![](/conversion-rate/assets/images/17.JPG)

...Their upcoming character, leaked through an upcoming livestream notification which at the time of writing, is not supposed to go live for another two days. The community has responded not with shock or amusement, but rather disbelief at the fact that this is *Dragon Ball Legends* **8th** major leak in recent history. Nothing like a character leaking prior to their announcement can absolutely stop the momentum for conversations to be had regarding the future and excitement of an upcoming announcement. Coming right off the heels of the overall well received 8th Anniversary, the hype leading into this announcement has already staled. It's fortunate that this wasn't a character or release that was highly anticipated during a significant celebration of the game...
